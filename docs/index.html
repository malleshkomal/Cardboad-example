Scope 1, 2 and 3 explained

The GHG Protocol sorts a company's emissions by who controls the source. Scope 1 and 2 are the company's own operations; Scope 3 is everything else in the value chain, upstream and downstream.

How supplier data flows into Scope 3

RequestSupplier survey + PCF request (PACT format) sent by purchasing.
Rate quality5 PACT indicators → data quality rating (DQR 1-5).
Accept / fall backDQR ≤ 3.0 → supplier PCF used, otherwise secondary database factor.
CalculateVolume × applied factor → Cat 1 emissions per supplier.
EngageScorecard rating A-D sets the action: roadmap, improvement plan or escalation.
